Dusk Market — Spot the Difference: two procedural-canvas pictures of a dusk market, side by side. Find all 7 differences before the 150-second timer runs out. Click a difference to mark it; a wrong click costs 5 seconds. The 7 differences: the lantern colour, the cat on the roof, an extra balloon, the awning stripe, the apples on the stall, the star on the sign, and the moon.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./output node main.ts
```

Then open `output/index.html` in a browser — or serve `game/` directly and open `game/index.html`. No network needed; everything runs offline.
